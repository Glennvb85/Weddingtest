Equivalent challenge steps

Formula
Steps = 160 × T × ( C / (11 × T) )¼

T = moving minutes, C = activity calories. The baseline is 160 steps/min at 11 kcal/min. The fourth-root intensity adjustment gives harder rides more credit without letting calorie estimates dominate.

Use moving time rather than elapsed time, and use the same calorie source consistently across the group.
